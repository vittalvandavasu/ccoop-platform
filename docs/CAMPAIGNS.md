# Campaign transitions (Prompt 2)

Drafts and scheduled campaigns are private to authorized campus curators/admins. Draft catalog edits replace SKUs only before publication and before any pledge/order exists. Published titles, price, media, thresholds and SKUs are immutable. Deadline extensions require a reason, expected revision and idempotency key; they never reopen a completed phase.

Publication is immediate or scheduled. Required windows are publication < pledge close < resolution end < payment end. A curator can explicitly close early; crossing MOQ never closes a campaign. Closing snapshots prices, category/color thresholds, size-aggregated color quantities, eligible colors, actor and time. No size has an independent MOQ.

Category eligibility uses **eligible-color units**, not failed-color demand. If all demand is below category MOQ or no color qualifies, closure fails immediately. Otherwise failed-color pledges enter SWITCH_REQUIRED and winning pledges wait in PENDING_RESOLUTION. Students explicitly switch to an eligible color at the snapshotted price (same quantity) or cancel; unresolved pledges expire at the resolution deadline. Once all failed-color pledges resolve, or time expires, eligible-color units are re-evaluated against category MOQ. The campaign becomes PAYMENT_OPEN or FAILED. This replaces the old immediate-payment interpretation when any color still needs resolution. Closed color eligibility is fixed; failed colors cannot be revived. Winning-color pledges cannot be edited/canceled after locking, so switches can only increase qualifying demand.

Before lock, students can change SKU/quantity or cancel free pledges; the 10-unit aggregate cap applies to active pledges. Mutation keys bind actor, operation and payload; expected revisions reject stale edits. Conversion snapshots price, SKU, quantity and eligibility. No catalog edit reprices existing orders or obligations. A failed/canceled/expired free pledge has no refund or credit entitlement.

Payment-window expiry changes the campaign to PAYMENT_CLOSED and expires unpaid invitations, never deleting/canceling orders or pending provider attempts. A timely verified payment delivered late can still credit during PAYMENT_CLOSED; a late completion is retained on hold under existing reconciliation policy. Production approval is a separate paid category/color MOQ gate and permits PAYMENT_OPEN or PAYMENT_CLOSED. Production never admits later initial payments.

All transitions lock campaign first, then pledge/order/obligation. BullMQ's recurring recovery job scans persisted deadlines and executes all overdue transitions in deadline order, including after downtime. Jobs are idempotent through persisted state under row locks; database/outbox/audit writes commit together. Dates, not job delivery time, decide admission. Outbox messages are the in-app inbox; external email/SMS is not delivered.

Existing published campaigns keep their commercial records. Legacy windows without resolution/payment dates receive conservative deadlines at first evaluation. Existing payment snapshots/provider identities are retained. Actual Cashfree sandbox rehearsal remains a payment-release gate.
